03 Performance

Fast despite a free-tier host

An image-heavy, animation-rich site is a bad match for a free-tier Render account unless the images stop being the bottleneck. I offloaded the site's images to Cloudinary CDN — served resized and compressed at the edge — so load times stayed fast while avoiding a hosting upgrade. Moving the site to Render also eliminated the hosting cost the client had been paying for their legacy site. I handled deployment end to end: shipped to Render, configured DNS, and managed client communication throughout.
